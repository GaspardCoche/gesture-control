How it works

Three steps. No setup.

From zero to gesture-controlled in under five seconds. Models load in the background while you read this.

1

Allow camera access

One-time browser prompt. The video feed stays on your machine — no upload, no telemetry.

2

Pick a mode

Inspect, Draw or Measure. Switch with the Peace gesture (held 1.5s) or keys 1/2/3.

3

Use your hands

Point to hover, pinch to act, swipe to scroll. Press V on a selected element to dictate feedback.

Features

Built for hands-free precision.

Real-time hand tracking, iris-based eye tracking, and offline speech transcription — all working together in your browser.

DOM Inspector

Point to hover any element. Pinch to lock-in and reveal computed styles, dimensions, and tag info.

Freehand Annotations

Pinch and move to draw directly on top of the page. Open hand ends the stroke. Z to undo.

Pixel Measurements

Pinch twice to drop two anchor points. The exact pixel distance appears live on the canvas.

Voice Feedback

Select an element, press V, and dictate. Outputs a structured AI-ready prompt with full element context.

Eye Tracking

Iris-based gaze cursor with Kalman-filtered smoothing. Blink to select. Toggle with thumbs up.

Touchless Scroll

Swipe down or up with an open hand to scroll one page at a time. Ballistic, snappy, no fatigue.

Gesture vocabulary

Eight gestures. Zero tutorials needed.

Each gesture has a stability gate to prevent accidental triggers. Hold a gesture for the system to lock it in.

Point
Move cursor, hover elements
Pinch
Select, draw, drop measure point
Open hand
Release, deselect, end stroke
Swipe
Quick down/up = page scroll
Peace (1.5s)
Switch to next mode
Fist (hold)
Clear all annotations
Thumbs up
Toggle eye tracking
V key + selection
Voice feedback recording

      await markPaid(event.data.object.id);
